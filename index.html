ℹ️ About STREAM 2026 & Project Information

Theme: Classroom to Cosmos · Category 2: AI Digital Solutions, Gamification & Cybersecurity · Organiser: School Education and Literacy Department, Government of Sindh · Class XI, Govt. Boys HSS Bhansinghabad, Mirpurkhas.

  1. Classroom to digital exploration: textbook ideas become experiments you can run.
  2. AI: students train and question a simple classifier and see why it decides.
  3. Gamification: XP, levels, badges and daily missions motivate practice.
  4. Cybersecurity: safe, responsible technology habits.
  5. Simulations: change mass, force, angle and gravity and observe results.
  6. 21st-century skills: problem solving, digital literacy, computational thinking.

Tech: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Canvas, localStorage. No server, database, API key or paid service. Works offline after first load.
